🔑 The 100-Doors Intuition Pump

With only 3 doors, intuition tricks us into thinking: "2 doors left = 50/50!" Scale the experiment to 100 doors to make the underlying mathematics intuitive:

Scale Total Doors: 100 Doors
Initial Pick (Stay) 1 / 100 (1.0%) You almost certainly picked a goat!
