🔑 The 100-Doors Trick (Why Switching Always Wins)

With only 3 doors, our intuition tricks us into thinking: "There are 2 doors left, so it must be 50/50!"
To see why that's wrong, imagine playing the exact same game with 100 doors instead:

Total Doors: 100 Doors
Your Initial Pick (Keep) 1 / 100 (1.0%) You almost certainly picked a goat!
The Host Removes 98 Goats The host intentionally skips the car.
If You Switch 99 / 100 (99.0%) Virtually guaranteed to win!
